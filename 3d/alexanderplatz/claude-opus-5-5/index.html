    About this model

    Every mesh, material, texture and sign in this scene is generated in code. There are no imported 3D models, photographs or map tiles, and the page makes no network requests at runtime.

    Data

    Building footprints and heights, streets, tram and rail lines, trees, lamps, benches and stops come from OpenStreetMap, © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License. The data was baked into the app at build time. One scene unit is one metre.

    Accuracy

    The Fernsehturm, Park Inn, Weltzeituhr, Alexanderplatz station, Rotes Rathaus and Marienkirche are hand-modelled from published dimensions and their OSM footprints. Overall heights and positions are close to reality; facades, ornament, window counts, signage and interior details are simplified or invented. Other buildings use generic facades chosen from their OSM tags. Traffic, timetables and crowd density are illustrative, not real.
