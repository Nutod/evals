      About this model

      What is real and what is approximate

      Every mesh, texture and sign here is generated in code at load time. Nothing is downloaded while you explore: no map tiles, no photos, no imported 3D models and no API keys.

      From OpenStreetMap

      Building footprints and tagged heights, streets, tram and Stadtbahn tracks, parks, water, trees, lamps, benches and stops were baked from OpenStreetMap into the app. One scene unit is one metre. Data snapshot: .

      Approximated

      The six landmarks use published overall dimensions on their mapped footprints, but facades, ornament, window counts, signage and interiors are simplified. Ordinary buildings get generic facades chosen from their tags. Vehicles, timetables, crowds, weather and lighting are illustrative. This is not a survey and should not be used for measurement.
